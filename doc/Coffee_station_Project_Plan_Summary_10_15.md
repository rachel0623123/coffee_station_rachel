# Coffee'station

## Bottom line

The purpose of this project is to build a simple, user-friendly, and convenient coffee ordering application.
The application allows customers to browse coffee products and place orders online quickly and easily. It also enables the cafe to showcase its coffee products on the website and introduce them to potential customers.
This platform consists of **React + Vite + TypeScript frontend**.

## Key points

- **Convenient and quick Booking & Ordering:** Allow customers to browse coffee products and place orders quickly and easily.
- **Showcasing Coffee Products:** Enable the cafe to display coffee images, names, descriptions, and prices on the website.
- **Filtering by Category:** Allow customers to filter coffee products by category to find their preferred drinks.
- **Shopping Cart Management:** Allow customers to add products, update quantities, remove items, and view the total price.
- **Checkout Simulation:** Provide an order summary and confirmation message without processing real payments.
- **Responsive Design:** Ensure the application works well on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Tested before delivery:** test cases, plus lint and build checks on every PR.
